Data

BGP updates stream live from RIPE NCC RIS Live (route collectors peering with hundreds of networks worldwide), filtered to the address space of the watched Malaysian networks. ASNs are verified against RIPEstat and PeeringDB, prefixes come from RIPEstat announced-prefixes, and the baseline is each RIS peer's actual route from RIPEstat bgp-state.

Detectors

Origin change / MOAS: a watched prefix announced by an AS that never originated it.
More-specific: a sub-prefix of watched space announced by another AS (the most dangerous hijack type).
RPKI invalid: checked locally against the rpki-client VRP set (RFC 6811).
Visibility drop: fewer baseline peers carry the prefix for longer than normal convergence.
Route leak (heuristic): valleys in the AS path, using CAIDA's transit-free clique and inferred relationships.

Alerting

Findings are grouped into incidents per network and suspect AS, so one event pages once. Weak evidence (fewer than 3 RIS peers) waits for confirmation and is shown at reduced severity. Incidents re-notify only on escalation, resolve when the anomaly disappears, and re-opening within the cooldown is silent.

Limits

BGP Watch only sees what RIS peers see, baselines are a few hours old, and route-leak checks depend on inferred relationships. Treat alerts as leads to investigate, not proof.
